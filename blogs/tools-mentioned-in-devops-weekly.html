continuous delivery of software platforms and artefacts. Unlike some similar tools it hands off much of the work to other lower-level platforms or command line tools and focuses on the integration piece.

distributed systems. It takes advantage of the capabilities of

Javascript.

https://spiffe.io/ https://github.com/spiffe/spiffe

What I Do

My core work is helping engineering teams ship software faster and more reliably. That means different things for different organisations — for some it's designing a CI/CD pipeline from scratch, for others it's untangling a Kubernetes cluster that's become impossible to operate, and for others it's building the internal practices (on-call, incident response, deployment standards) that let teams scale without burning out.

On the security side, I integrate DevSecOps into the delivery process rather than bolting it on at the end — SAST/DAST in CI pipelines, secrets management with HashiCorp Vault, container image scanning, and compliance programmes (ISO 27001, GDPR, PCI DSS). I led this work at Aigent for enterprise clients across multiple regions.

I also teach. Since 2022 I've been a visiting lecturer at MIT World Peace University and IMCC Pune, covering DevOps Engineering, Cloud Computing, and Server Management. I've mentored 3,000+ students. I run training workshops for engineering teams and have organised DevSecOps Pune, one of the city's active security meetup communities.
